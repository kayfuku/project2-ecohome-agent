Heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) typically accounts for 40-50% of a home's energy use, making it the single biggest opportunity for savings. Small changes to thermostat settings, maintenance habits and scheduling can reduce HVAC costs by 15-30% without sacrificing comfort.

Thermostat Setpoints:
- Set heating to 68°F when home and awake, and 60-62°F while asleep or away
- Set cooling to 78°F when home and 82-85°F while away
- Each 1°F adjustment over 8 hours saves roughly 1% on heating or cooling costs
- Avoid large setpoint swings; recovering from extreme setbacks can waste energy

Pre-Conditioning with Time-of-Use Rates:
- Pre-cool the home by 2-3°F before peak pricing hours begin (often 4 PM to 9 PM)
- Let the temperature drift upward during peak hours, then resume normal cooling
- Pre-heat during off-peak morning hours in winter when rates are lower
- Use solar generation hours (10 AM to 3 PM) to run the HVAC when panels are producing

Heat Pump Tips:
- Avoid frequent manual setpoint changes that trigger inefficient backup resistance heat
- Use "auto" fan mode instead of "on" to reduce fan energy use
- Keep outdoor units clear of snow, leaves and debris

Maintenance:
- Replace or clean air filters every 1-3 months
- Schedule a professional tune-up once a year before the heating or cooling season
- Keep supply and return vents unblocked by furniture or rugs
- Seal and insulate ducts in attics and crawl spaces, which can lose 20-30% of airflow

Zoning and Airflow:
- Close doors and adjust vents in unused rooms only if your system supports zoning
- Use ceiling fans to feel 4°F cooler in summer; reverse direction in winter to push warm air down
