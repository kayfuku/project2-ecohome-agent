Smart home automation lets your devices respond to schedules, occupancy, electricity prices and weather without manual effort. Well-designed automations can cut household energy use by 10-20% while making the home more comfortable and convenient.

Smart Thermostats:
- Enable geofencing so the thermostat switches to away mode when everyone leaves
- Use learning or adaptive schedules that adjust to your daily routine
- Connect the thermostat to your utility's demand response program for bill credits
- Review monthly energy reports to spot unusual HVAC runtime

Smart Plugs and Power Strips:
- Put entertainment centers, game consoles and office equipment on smart plugs to cut standby power
- Schedule plugs to turn off overnight and while you are at work
- Use energy-monitoring plugs to identify devices with high phantom load

Lighting Automation:
- Install occupancy or vacancy sensors in bathrooms, closets, garages and hallways
- Use daylight sensors to dim lights when natural light is sufficient
- Create "away" and "goodnight" scenes that turn off all lights with one command

Price and Solar-Aware Automations:
- Trigger dishwashers, washing machines and EV chargers when electricity prices drop
- Run flexible loads when solar generation exceeds household consumption
- Receive alerts when peak pricing periods are about to begin

Best Practices:
- Start with a few high-impact automations and expand gradually
- Use a central hub or app so devices from different brands work together
- Keep firmware updated for security and efficiency improvements
- Add manual overrides so automations never compromise comfort or safety
